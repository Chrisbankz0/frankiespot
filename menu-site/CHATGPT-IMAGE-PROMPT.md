# Prompt for ChatGPT — food photos for the Frankies Pot menu

This generates 96 images, one per item, each already matched to the exact
filename `menu.js` and `images/README.txt` expect. Read **How to use** first —
it's short, and it's the difference between 96 photos that look like one
consistent menu and 96 that look like they came from 96 different kitchens.

## How to use this

1. Open a **new** ChatGPT chat (a plus/paid account with image generation).
   Do everything below in this one chat, in order. ChatGPT uses earlier
   images in the same chat as a style reference — that's the only real lever
   you have for keeping 96 photos looking like they belong on one menu.
2. Paste the **Style guide** block first, on its own. Look at the image it
   makes before continuing — regenerate it until the plate, background and
   lighting are right, because everything after this builds on it.
3. Then paste each category's item prompts in order, one at a time, waiting
   for the image before sending the next. Each prompt names the exact file
   the photo should become.
4. Download each image, rename it to the filename given, and drop it into
   the `images/` folder, replacing nothing else. Refresh the page and it
   appears.
5. If one comes out wrong, reply "regenerate this one, keep the same style"
   rather than starting over.

96 is a lot. Realistically, most kitchens don't shoot every single one before
launching. A sane order of priority: the 4 items marked * (the two proteins
that anchor the combo section, plus the plainest side and the most-ordered
soup), then the rest of the Rice & Spaghetti Combo section, since that's
almost certainly where most orders come from, then Sides, then work down from
there whenever there's time. The page never breaks with a photo missing — it
just shows a plain lettered tile until you add one.

## Style guide — paste this first

```
I'm building a set of food photos for a Nigerian restaurant menu called
Frankies Pot. I need many images in total, all in one consistent style, so
I'm going to describe the style once here and then ask for each dish one at
a time — please keep every image after this one matching this exact style,
as if the same photographer shot all of them in the same sitting.

STYLE:
- Realistic food photography, not illustration, not 3D render, not painted.
- Shot from directly overhead (flat lay / top-down), centred in the frame.
- Square 1:1 image.
- The food is served on a plain white or off-white ceramic plate (or bowl,
  for soups), filling most of the frame with a small margin of background
  showing.
- Background is a plain, slightly textured surface — light wood or a warm
  neutral cloth — nothing patterned, nothing branded, no other objects.
- Soft, natural daylight, no harsh flash, no visible light source, gentle
  shadows.
- No text, no logos, no watermarks, no hands, no people, no cutlery unless I
  ask for it.
- Colours should look true to Nigerian home cooking — rich, warm, a little
  glossy from oil, not oversaturated or artificial-looking.

First image, to set the reference: a plate of Nigerian party jollof rice
with a piece of roasted chicken, shot in exactly this style. Generate that.
```

## A note on cost and limits
Image generation in ChatGPT is rate-limited even on paid plans — 96 in one
sitting is unlikely. If it slows down or asks you to wait, come back to the
same chat later and keep going from where you stopped. The style guide image
stays in the chat's memory the whole time, so a break in the middle won't
break the consistency.

A faster option throughout: shoot the real thing for anything you're already
cooking today, and only lean on ChatGPT to fill the combinations that would
otherwise mean replating the same pot of soup or stew a dozen times just for
a photo. Real photos of your actual food will always read as more trustworthy
to a customer than AI-generated ones.
