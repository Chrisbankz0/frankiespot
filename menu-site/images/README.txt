A NOTE ON THE RICE & SPAGHETTI COMBO SECTION
----------------------------------------------

That section alone is 60 photos, because every base (jollof, white rice,
spaghetti) and every side option is its own line and its own photo slot.
A shortcut worth knowing: the protein and sides look close to identical
across the three bases. If you plate one full spread — beef, beef with
plantain, beef with egg sauce, beef with both — on jollof, then re-plate
the exact same four combinations on white rice and again on spaghetti,
you get all 12 "beef" photos in three sittings instead of twelve.


THE RULES
---------

Shape     Square. The page crops to a square either way, so shooting
          square saves you surprises.

Size      Around 800 x 800 pixels. Anything bigger only makes the page
          slower on mobile data.

Weight    Under about 150KB each. Squoosh.app shrinks a phone photo to a
          fraction of its size with no visible loss. This matters more
          than it sounds: a customer on a weak connection gives up before
          a 4MB photo loads. With close to 100 photos on one page, this
          one really counts.

Format    .jpg for photos.

Naming    Copy the names above exactly. Lowercase, hyphens, no spaces,
          and .jpg not .JPG. A capital letter is enough to break the link
          on a live server, even though it works on your laptop.

            good  ->  jollof-beef-combo.jpg
            bad   ->  Jollof Beef Combo (2).JPG

SHOOTING THEM YOURSELF
----------------------

Daylight near a window, no flash, phone held directly above the plate.
Plain background: a wooden table or a plain cloth. Shoot the food as it
actually arrives, not styled beyond recognition, or you get complaints.

There's also a ready-made prompt for generating these with ChatGPT if you'd
rather not shoot all 96 by hand — see CHATGPT-IMAGE-PROMPT.md in this
folder. It'll need updating to match this new list before it's accurate;
ask for that whenever you're ready to use it.
